The curriculum documents behind the taxonomy. Codes were constructed in document order (except the KMK digital-world framework, which has official numbering); titles are short paraphrases, not official wording. Click a row to see its codes and the topics linked to them.

Research prototype, authored with AI assistance and not yet reviewed by German teachers. Klasse 7–13 follow the Gymnasium in G9 numbering (in G8 Länder everything from Klasse 7 happens about a year earlier); Oberstufe topics are marked gA (all courses) or eA (Leistungskurs only). Grade placement follows the typical or majority placement across the Länder; check the Varies by Land notes and your own Land's plan.
