Lernlandkarte · learning map

What children learn at school, and what each step builds on

Every topic of the German and the Romanian curriculum, from the first school year to the Abitur and the Bacalaureat, placed in the year it starts and linked to the topics a child needs first. Built from the official standards and curricula, and compared with each other and with Marble's os-taxonomy.

Research prototype, written with AI assistance and not yet reviewed by teachers. In Germany it follows the Gymnasium (G9) from Klasse 7; in Romania the theoretical liceu.

Germany

  • 3D map Every topic as a dot and every prerequisite as a thread, rising through the school years. Click a topic to light up everything that comes before it, or switch to os-taxonomy to compare the shapes. Open the map →
  • Taxonomy browser A curriculum map per subject, a searchable list of all topics, learning paths, summaries for parents and the curriculum sources behind each topic. Browse the taxonomy →
  • Comparison with os-taxonomy Which German topics have a counterpart, and whether German children meet an idea earlier or later than children in England, the US and Australia. Open the comparison →

Romania

  • 3D map The Romanian curriculum on the same age scale, from clasa pregătitoare to clasa a XII-a. Switch between the Romanian and German maps to compare their shapes. Open the map →
  • Taxonomy browser A curriculum map per subject, all topics, learning paths, parent summaries and the programe școlare behind each topic, with the liceu specialisation that teaches it. Browse the taxonomy →
  • Romania vs Germany Which topics the two curricula share, which only one of them teaches, and whether Romanian children meet an idea earlier or later than German children. Open the comparison →

What's inside

  • Germany: 18 subjects, Vorschule–Klasse 13, KMK standards and Länder curricula
  • Romania: clasa pregătitoare–a XII-a, national programe școlare
  • Names in German or Romanian and English

Every page has a Core only switch that hides the second foreign languages, Latin, the arts, sport, religion and (in Romania) technology and personal development.

Data and source code: github.com/codingarchitect-wq/lernlandkarte.
