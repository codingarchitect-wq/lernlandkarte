Lernlandkarte · learning map

What children learn at German schools, and what each step builds on

Every topic from Vorschule to the Abitur, placed in the school year it starts and linked to the topics a child needs first. Built from the KMK educational standards and the curricula of the Länder, and compared with Marble's os-taxonomy.

Research prototype, written with AI assistance and not yet reviewed by German teachers. From Klasse 7 it follows the Gymnasium (G9).

  • 3D map Every topic as a dot and every prerequisite as a thread, rising through the school years. Click a topic to light up everything that comes before it, or switch to os-taxonomy to compare the shapes. Open the map →
  • Taxonomy browser A curriculum map per subject, a searchable list of all topics, learning paths, summaries for parents and the curriculum sources behind each topic. Browse the taxonomy →
  • Comparison with os-taxonomy Which German topics have a counterpart, and whether German children meet an idea earlier or later than children in England, the US and Australia. Open the comparison →

What's inside

  • 18 subjects, from Mathematik to Latein
  • Vorschule–Klasse 13, with Grund- and Leistungskurs
  • KMK standards and Länder curricula
  • German and English names

Every page has a Core only switch that hides Französisch, Latein, Musik, Kunst, Sport and Religion & Ethik.

Data and source code: github.com/codingarchitect-wq/lernlandkarte.
